> **Reading Level Target:** Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level $\le$ 8.0 (First-year collegiate reading ease).

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Testing Objectives

In collegiate placement operations, ambiguous notification phrasing leads to panic, missed interviews, and flooded placement cell queues. The Institutional Placement Engine (IM-101) enforces a **Three-Part Clarity Standard** for all student status explanations:
1. **What the status is** (Clear verdict in plain language).
2. **Why** (Specific numeric or factual threshold comparison).
3. **What to do next** (Clear, actionable student next step).

This kit documents the formal usability test script administered to **three representative students** to validate reading level, identify confusing phrasing, and prove 3 of 3 comprehension on mobile viewports.

## 3. Test Script & Protocol

### Facilitator Instructions:
1. Hand the student a mobile device (390x844 viewport) running the student portal.
2. Present one real status explanation card at a time.
3. Ask the two standardized evaluation questions:
   - **Question 1:** *"In your own words, what does this status line mean for your participation?"*
   - **Question 2:** *"What exact action should you take right now based on this message?"*
4. Record raw student answers verbatim.
5. Score comprehension:
   - **Pass (1.0):** Correct meaning and actionable next step identified immediately.
   - **Partial (0.5):** Understood status but confused about what to do.
   - **Fail (0.0):** Misinterpreted eligibility or panicked.

## 5. Iterative Wording Refinements (Before vs. After)

During pilot testing with draft copy, two phrases caused brief pauses. The table below documents how student feedback drove improved wording:

| Scenario | Initial Draft Phrasing *(Caused Pause)* | Student Confusion Point | Final Production Wording *(3/3 Pass)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dream Upgrade** | *"Policy tier violation: Candidate holds Tier-2 offer. Dream upgrade threshold R-04 requires $\ge$ 10 LPA."* | Student asked: *"What is R-04? Does this mean I am disqualified permanently?"* | *"You cannot sit for this drive: you already have an offer, and this company is not in the dream-company category. Keep working hard in your selected company or wait for a dream drive offering 10.0 LPA or above."* |
| **Backlog History** | *"Ineligible due to standing arrear count = 1. Arrear history tolerance exceeded."* | Student asked: *"Does 'arrear' mean history or active? I already passed it last month."* | *"You are not eligible: you have 1 active backlog and this company allows at most 0. Clear your pending subject in the upcoming supplementary examination cycle."* |
| **Venue Notice** | *"Allocated slot 402 at CS_LAB_02 on 2026-10-27T10:00:00. Report -15m."* | Difficult to read on a mobile phone in bright sunlight. | *"You are eligible. Your test is on Tuesday at 10:00 in Lab 2. Be present 15 minutes before time with your college ID card."* |

## 7. Ongoing Usability Maintenance Protocol

To ensure future announcement templates or policy edits maintain this clarity:
1. **Never commit a status template without all 3 parts:**
   - [ ] Part 1: What is the status?
   - [ ] Part 2: Why? (exact number or rule)
   - [ ] Part 3: What can the student do next?
2. Run automated readability validation via `calculateReadingLevel()` in `frontend/src/utils/student_status_templates.ts`.
3. Check touch targets on a physical phone or Playwright mobile viewport (`width: 390, height: 844`): all buttons, tabs, and input controls must measure $\ge 44\text{px}$.
